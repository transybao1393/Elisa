# Elisa brand guide

Elisa is a local coding assistant for the Mac: a dark, terminal-like window with pixel labels (style 2h) and a companion, Shade, who shows what the model is doing.

## Principles

- Calm by default. Two fonts, one accent. Pink = Elisa and you; cyan = model activity and code; green/red/amber = outcomes only.
- Pixel labels, mono everything. Silkscreen for short uppercase labels, Chivo Mono for all reading text.
- The character is a status light. Every pose maps to an app state (07-motion/state-mapping.md). It pops only when the user is needed or something finished: greeting, permission, celebrating, confused, error.

## Voice

Short and factual. Status reads like a terminal: `thinking · 2.1K ctx · 34 tok/s`. Bubble verbs are one uppercase word.

## Marks

- App icon: 06-icons/app (16–1024, plus concepts a–c).
- Menu bar: 06-icons/menubar and 05-character/shade/svg/elisa-shade-menubar-*.svg.
- Clear space ¼ of the mark width. Don't recolour the character outside its palette, add a mouth, or place it on busy imagery.

## Themes

Dark (default) and light share one structure. See 02-color.
