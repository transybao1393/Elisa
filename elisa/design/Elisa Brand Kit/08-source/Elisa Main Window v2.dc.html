YOUwhat's the practical difference between a mutex and a semaphore?
ELISA
A mutex guards one resource: one holder at a time, and only the holder unlocks it. A semaphore is a counter of permits: up to N holders at once, and any task can release a permit.
–Use a mutex to protect shared state.
–Use a semaphore to cap concurrency (connections, workers, downloads).
