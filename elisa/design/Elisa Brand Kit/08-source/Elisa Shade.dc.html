Shade: character and motion

A small ink-black creature that lives on your Mac. Two big pale eyes do all the talking: there is no mouth, so pupils, lids and fox ears carry every state. Cyan shows inside the ears, the same cyan the app uses for model activity. Twelve core states plus eight actions, one renderer: every animation on this page is driven by the same keyframe tracks that ship as JSON.

01

Character

BodySoft hooded shape, 104 wide on a 200 grid, sitting on (100, 172). Ink #141418 with a #3A3A46 rim; the rim is what holds the silhouette on dark windows.
EyesTwo large pale ovals, 42 × 48, with dark pupils and one highlight. Pupils follow the look tracks with a slight upward bias, so it always reads as curious.
ExpressionNo mouth. Open, wide (smaller pupils), happy arcs, closed, sad (outer lids droop). Eyes glow cyan on memory recall.
EarsTall fox ears, cyan inside, pivoting at the base. Perk, flick, fold outward, droop.
PawsSecondary motion only. Dropped below 32 px.
MiniAt 24 px and below the body lifts to #3A3A46 with no rim so it doesn’t vanish on dark menus; eyes keep the pupils, lose the highlight.
EffectsCyan for model activity (thinking, tools, memory). Green, red, amber only for outcome badges.

04

Motion spec

Rules

Frame rate60 fps authored; 30 fps is fine at 24 px and below.
PivotBody scales and tilts from the bottom centre (100, 172), so it stays planted.
Squash0.86 – 1.12 on y, with x counter-scaled. Volume roughly preserved.
Blink3 frames close, 2 hold, 3 open (≈ 120 ms). Once per idle loop.
TransitionsBlend any two poses over 200 ms ease in-out. Sleeping → waking uses its own clip.
Reduced motionShow the key pose for each state; crossfade 150 ms between them.
