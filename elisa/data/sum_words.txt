# How arithmetic is said in words (`mcp_reason::ops_in_words`): the wordings that stand between two numbers for one
# of `calc`'s operators, or around one number for a percentage, a square, a cube or a square root, so Elisa computes
# "144 chia 12" and "25% của 480" herself as she does "144 divided by 12" and "25% of 480", whatever the model says.
# One section per operator, "a | b | c", English and Vietnamese, with and without accents; a wording may be several
# words. Only wordings that mean the operator between two numbers belong here (not "lần" as "occasion" alone, not a
# bare "căn"/"can"). Fix a missed wording here, not in code.

[plus]
plus | added to | cộng | cong | cộng với | cong voi | cộng thêm | cong them | thêm | them

[minus]
minus | trừ | tru | trừ đi | tru di | bớt | bot | bớt đi | bot di

[times]
times | multiplied by | nhân | nhan | nhân với | nhan voi | lần | lan

[divided by]
divided by | chia | chia cho

[to the power of]
to the power of | mũ | mu | lũy thừa | luy thua
