# Words that ask for another language or a script (`reply_check::asks_other_language`): an English message holding one
# as a whole word ("translate good morning", "how do you say cat in Japanese?") may get a reply in that language, so
# the reply-language check leaves it alone. One key per line, "key: wording | wording | …", in an [en] and a [vi]
# section, with and without accents. Fix a missed wording here, not in code.
#   asks:  verbs and words that ask for words in another language
#   asks_after: the same, standing right after the language's name ("'tôi đói rồi' tiếng Anh là gì?")
#   names: names of languages and scripts
#   vietnamese: names of Vietnamese, for an ask wanting Vietnamese words (`reply_check::garbled` checks their syllables)
#   english: names of English, for an ask wanting English words (`reply_check::translation_missing`: a reply with no
#            English in it is no translation)
#   into:  the word before the language asked for ("to English", "sang tiếng Anh"): with two languages named, the one
#          after it is the target (`reply_check::translation_target`)
#   order: wordings that open an instruction to answer in a language ("reply in", "trả lời bằng"): a reply sentence
#          opening with one and naming a language (`names`) or the message's language (`order_of`) right after it is
#          Elisa's own language note said back, and goes (`reply_check::without_lang_orders`)
#   order_of: the words after an `order` wording that name the message's language ("the language of")
