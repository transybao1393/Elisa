# A clock time plus or minus a duration, worked out by Elisa herself (`situation` `clock_sum`): "a train leaves at
# 3:20 pm and the trip takes 2 hours 55 minutes. when does it arrive?" is 6:15 pm (moondream:1.8b said 7:55 pm). The
# start time and the duration are read from the user's words with these wordings; a reply's time that is not the
# result (nor the start, the duration or a step on the way) is put right. One wording per line as "key: wording",
# lowercase, matched as whole words; add a language's wording here, not in code.
#   hours, minutes   the units of a duration ("2 hours 55 minutes", "2 tiếng 55 phút", "90 min")
#   join             what may stand between a duration's hours and minutes ("2 hours and 55 minutes")
#   oclock           what follows the hour of a time said with it ("lúc 3 giờ 20 chiều", "at 3 o'clock"): in the
#                    user's words such a time counts only right after an `at` word (else "2 giờ" is a duration)
#   at               a word right before a start time said with `oclock`
#   am, pm           the 12-hour marks ("3:20 pm", "9 am"): the result is said the same way
#   am part, pm part the parts of the day said after a time instead ("15:20", "3:20 chiều"): the result is said
#                    as a 24-hour time
#   later            a word that makes the duration come after the start ("after", "2 hours later", "sau")
#   later right before   a later word only when it comes right before the duration ("in 2 hours")
#   earlier          a word that makes it come before ("before", "ago", "when should I leave", "trước")
#   span             a word that says the duration is how long something takes ("takes", "lasts", "mất"): after
#                    the start, unless an `earlier` word says otherwise
#   asks             the words that ask for the time (besides a question mark)
#   working.<lang>   Elisa's working, added to a reply that states no time: {start} {sign} {duration} = {result}
#   hm.<lang>, h.<lang>, m.<lang>   a duration as said in that working ({h} hours, {m} minutes)
#   next day.<lang>, day before.<lang>   said after a result past midnight
