# A question about one thing Elisa can do ("can you search the web?", "bạn có đọc được file trên máy mình không?")
# and her own answer to it (`situation::ability_answer`; the routing's side is data/ability_asks.txt). The yes or no
# comes from what she holds, never from a fixed list: her tool list (a `[kind tool …]` section names the tools that
# give that kind of ability) and the model's detected facts (`[vision]`: whether the model sees pictures). The words
# are the user's own: the ability as they wrote it, said back with the pronouns swapped ("my" → "your").
#
# Frames, under `[en]` and `[vi]`: "question frame = answer when she can | answer when she cannot", {x} the ability.
# A Vietnamese frame matches a message written with or without its marks. `swap` lists the pronoun swaps (both ways
# at once: "me > you" and "you > me"), `tail` the politeness words dropped from the end of {x}.
# Kinds: a section per kind of ability with example wordings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, English and Vietnamese): an ability
# nearest a kind's example, near enough, nearer than any `[other]` example and sharing a word with that example, is
# that kind; one nearest `[other]` (chat: writing, explaining, translating) is left to the model's reply.
# The general question ("what can you do?", "bạn làm được gì cho tôi?": `situation::can_do_line`) gets her own list in
# the user's language: the `can_do` frame with `{list}`: the `always_first` wordings (chat), then the wording named for
# each kind she has here (a kind is hers by its tools; `vision` by the model's detected sight), then what plugin tools
# of no kind do, then the `always_last` wordings (attached documents, the clock: hers on every model), joined with the
# `and` word. A kind she lacks is never listed, so nothing here is a fixed claim. The same list goes to the model in
# the situation note of such a turn (`situation::about`).
# `who` lists the wordings of a question about who she is, not what she can do ("who are you?"): such a question
# never gets the list. Matched folded, so the wordings without marks need no line of their own.
# Fix a missed or wrong wording by adding an example or a frame here, not in code.

tail = please | pls | plz | too | as well
