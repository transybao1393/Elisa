# Elisa's own sentence for a fact she computed exactly from a table (`situation::table_facts`, the "Elisa computed it
# exactly from sales.csv: highest units = South (340) · …" note), when she answers from the results herself
# (`repl::turn` `computed_table_line`). One line per kind of fact, keyed by the words the note puts before the column
# ("highest units" → highest), English and Vietnamese. {label}: the row (for highest and lowest), {column}: the column
# as the table names it, {value}: the computed number, {file}: the table's name. A kind with no line here keeps the
# note's own words. ties: the word joining tied rows' labels (the note joins them with " and "); "{kind} tie": the
# kind's sentence for tied rows, where the language says it otherwise (else the kind's own line).
[en]
highest: {label} has the highest {column} in {file} ({value}).
lowest: {label} has the lowest {column} in {file} ({value}).
highest tie: {label} have the highest {column} in {file} ({value}).
lowest tie: {label} have the lowest {column} in {file} ({value}).
total: The total {column} in {file} is {value}.
average: The average {column} in {file} is {value}.
number of: {file} has {value} {column}.
ties: and
