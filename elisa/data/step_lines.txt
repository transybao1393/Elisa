# Elisa's own line for a step she ran, in the user's language, when she tells the results herself ("Here's what I
# found:" / "Đây là kết quả:" and then one line per step: `repl/turn.rs` `plain_results`, `reply_check::step_line`).
# One key per line, "key: line", in an [en] and a [vi] section; the key is the step's group as the activity rows
# count it (`activity::Phrase::group`: read, look, search_project, edit, create, run, tests, build, install, lint,
# git, doc, web_search, web_fetch, code, calc, time, sec, think, plan; "plugin" for any plugin's tool), "{target}" is
# what the step acted on (a file, a query, a command). A group with no line here gets the activity row's own words.
#   failed:  the step did not work ("{act}" is the step's live wording, "Reading notes.txt", English; a Vietnamese
#            line names the target instead)
#   skipped: the step was said no to
#   stopped: the step was cancelled
# A line that needs "{target}" where the step has none is not used (the activity row's words then).
