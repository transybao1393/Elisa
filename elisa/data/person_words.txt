# Who is who in a question about the user (`context::NameFix::about_user`): a message asking about the user's own
# facts ("what do you know about me?", "tóm tắt những gì bạn biết về mình", "tên mình là gì") whose reply describes
# the assistant instead (gemma3:1b read "mình" as itself) gets Elisa's answer from the user's facts. In Vietnamese
# "mình" in the user's message is the user, the assistant being "bạn". Fix a wording here, not in code.
# Keys per language:
#   me         the user's words for themself          about me   asks about the user as a whole
#   you        the words a reply speaks to the user with: a fact of theirs said with one is an answer
#   ask        words that make a message a question (with "?" or without: "mình sống ở đâu")
# One fact is asked when a "me" word stands right next to its thing ("my name", "tên mình", "mình sống").
#   say        Elisa's answer: {facts} the facts       fact       one fact "The user's X is V": {thing} {value}
#   things     a fact's thing in this language, "english: this language" (a thing not listed keeps its words)
#   lives / works   "The user lives in …" / "works …" in this language: {what} all after the verb ("in Hanoi"),
#              {place} without its English place word ("Hanoi"); the template's second word is the verb an ask uses
#   restate    lead-ins of a sentence that says something over again ("as for", "in other words"): a reply sentence
#              opening with one that tells the user a fact of theirs an earlier sentence told goes (`NameFix::fix`)

[en]
me = i | me | my | myself | mine
you = you | your | yours | you're | you've
about me = about me | about myself
ask = what | which | where | who | how | when | tell
say = Here is what I know about you: {facts}
fact = Your {thing} is {value}.
lives = You live {what}.
works = You work {what}.
restate = as for | as to | regarding | when it comes to | in other words | that is to say | to put it another way | as i said | as i mentioned | like i said | as mentioned | as noted | to repeat | once again | again
